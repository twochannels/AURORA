AURORA

Big, wide sounds that keep moving on their own. The sky above is the sound, drawn live, the way the FM-1's screen draws it.

A custom firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1: a pad of sine partials that tilts, spreads and drifts, a swarm of saws under it, wind and crackle over it, a plate that goes on for ever, a tape delay, a freeze that holds the moment in grains, and the Circuit Tracks' eight macros as eight named controls.

A sound module for playing, not a groovebox: your sequencer sequences, AURORA sounds. Every page of its screen is a picture the four knobs shift.

Early days. The pad, the swarm, the air, the movement, the plate, the delay, the freeze and twelve presets are in; it is all in, and growing.

Hear it

Two and a half minutes, rendered by the firmware itself: six long chords in D minor on the AURORA sound, a swell, the air and the movement waking; then PLUCK over its echo; then one last chord frozen as the hands leave. Download the video or the sound alone.

The sky is the sound

HOME is an aurora. Three curtains of light cross the screen, bent live by the instrument's own movement sources: the random walk, the chaos and the tide that bend the sound bend the sky. What you have set colours it and shapes it. TILT sets the green from pale to deep, SPREAD the colour at the top from blue to red, BRIGHT the height, WIDTH the wavelength, RATE the drift. SPACE lays a haze over it, AIR puts sparks in it and sharpens the rays, FREEZE ices it and stops the drift. Every held note is a ray at its pitch, and the whole sky glows with the envelopes.

Under it, the four knobs for playing: TILT SPACE AIR FREEZE. A preset's name is the title, starred once you have changed the sound.

Every page is a picture

The four knobs have four colours, green, blue, violet and amber from left to right, and on every page each part of the picture is drawn in the colour of the knob that moves it. Turn a knob and watch its part of the picture change. The buttons step through their groups: EDIT for the tone, ENV for the envelope and the touch, LFO for the movement, FX for the space.

Twelve places to start

Turn the PRESETS encoder, on any page, or send a program change. From the slow and vast to the short and struck, and sixteen USER slots after them for your own:

AURORAthe opening sound: wide, echoing, shimmering, half frozen
DEEPsix seconds to arrive, half a minute to leave, everything moving
TIDEthe tide driving the space over minutes
CHOIRdetuned pairs, a little swarm under, wide
ICEbright and hollow, the air crackling, a shimmer an octave up
STORMthe air at full, chaos into the tone, tape echo feeding back
SWARMthe saws loud and resonant, every partial drifting
SUBthe fundamental alone over a low swarm
ORGANinstant on, instant off, full even partials
GLASSa struck bell, odd partials, falling while held
MALLETdarker and shorter, with a tape echo
PLUCKthe shortest, swarm-bright, an echo behind it

Install

From the install page in Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 on its USB cable, or from the FM-1 Firmware Hub. The rescue, holding OCT− at power-on, is always there, and the official firmware and any other custom firmware install over AURORA the same way.

In a dawless rig

Plug the sequencer's MIDI out into the FM-1's TRS jack (the FM-1 is TRS type A). Set the channel on the MIDI page, or leave it at OMNI. Hold chords from the sequencer, send the macros from its knobs, and let the movement do the rest.
