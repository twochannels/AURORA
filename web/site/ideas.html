What is asked for, and where it stands

Players ask for things, and some of them are better than what was planned. This page keeps the list in the open: what is next, what is planned, what is being thought about, and what was decided against and why. To add to it, open an issue.

The sound

planned
SLICE

A rhythmic gate on the whole sound, synced to the MIDI clock like the delay: depth, a note division, the edge from soft to hard, a few patterns and one that drifts. Before the delay and the plate, so the tail fills the gaps. A fourth FX page.

asked for by a player on Reddit

planned
MONO

One voice, last note wins, GLIDE as legato, saved with the sound: for basses and leads that crunch as chords. A switch on the MIDI page beside KEYS.

asked for by veecheech

planned
FOLD

A wavefolder on the METAL page: where DRIVE flattens the peaks, this bends them back, growing inharmonic overtones the harder you play. The scream under a horn.

planned
RESO

The air through a resonator tuned to the note: breath through a pipe, a bow on a string, a struck drone. The other empty knob on METAL.

planned
More presets on METAL

An anvil, a scraped drone, a riser, once FOLD and RESO are in.

Scenes and sounds

thinking
A and B, mixed and matched

Each sound would carry its own B, so a pair travels together, and you could still point B at any other preset or USER slot, A or B side. Today B is one setting for the whole instrument, picked with ARP and PRESETS. The question is what the PRESETS encoder shows when every slot has two halves.

asked for by a player

thinking
A sound sequence

The morph with more stops: a chain of scenes, each held for a time and crossfaded into the next, so sounds come in and go out on their own over a held chord. AURORA stays a sound module (your sequencer plays the notes); this would sequence the sound, not the notes. The step clock could follow the MIDI clock like the delay. The open question is how it is edited on four knobs.

asked for by grufallokoster on Reddit

planned
REVERSE

FREEZE's grains played backwards, and a reverse on the delay: the sound sequenced in reverse, swelling up to where it was struck. Cheap, since the grains already read from the delay line.

asked for by grufallokoster on Reddit

thinking
Send a pair

SAVE then GLO sends the scene you are at. A pair dump would send A and B together, marked, so a two-layer sound travels in one message and one issue.

planned
A sound back in

The share page sending a sound to the FM-1, and the emulator taking the text Copy sound makes, so sounds go both ways and can be heard before they are installed.

The instrument

thinking
A plugin

The engine is plain C and already runs on a computer and in the browser. A VST3, AU and standalone wrapper would run it at 44.1 kHz and resample, with every control automatable and the screen as the GUI. Signing for Macs is the awkward part.

thinking
Bluetooth MIDI

The FM-1's radio can do BLE MIDI with an open stack at a modest cost in flash. One link, notes and CCs, no audio. Worth it for a phone or a tablet as the controller.

thinking
A battery meter

The battery's voltage is on a pin nothing reads. A mark on the MIDI page.

not this
Wi-Fi

Needs the closed Wi-Fi libraries, most of the remaining flash and memory, and giving up USB audio. Not for this firmware.

not this
Choosing a waveshape

The pad is built from sine overtones; TILT, SPREAD and BRIGHT already are the shape, and METAL, DRIVE and the swarm add what a saw or square would. A waveform switch would add nothing the knobs do not.

Recently done

0.7.11
Saved sounds survive new controls

USER slots are read by the length of the sound when they were written, so a new control loads at its default instead of wiping the bank. Slots from 0.7.10 carry over.

0.7.10
Knobs step by one

A click on its own moves one, quick clicks move two, so every value is reachable; detents are learned slowly so a click never counts twice.
